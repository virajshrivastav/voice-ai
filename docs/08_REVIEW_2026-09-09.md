## Code verdict (read, not skimmed)
- **Keep:** script, answer_bank, guard, state_machine, classify, prerender, session,
  audio, costs. Pure, tested, encode real product decisions.
- **Patch:** vendor adapters (done for TTS; Sarvam STT is batch-only and a live call needs
  streaming — use Pipecat's `SarvamSTTService` or Gnani Prisma).
- **Replace for the phone:** `runner.CallRunner` is a synchronous half-duplex loop; a
  telephony bot is async/frame-driven with barge-in. Keep `Conversation`, drive it from a
  Pipecat processor. Pilot endpointing: 2.5 s ends a voter turn, 8 s → re-ask, 300 s cap.
- **Defer:** `campaign/`, `insights/` — not needed for call #1.
- Known nit: `AnswerBank.content_hash` docstring says placeholder fill is excluded; it is
  not (entries are filled before hashing), so changing `OFFICE_CONTACT` invalidates an
  approval. Decide which behaviour is wanted before the first real approval.
